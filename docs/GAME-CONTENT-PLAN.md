## Quiz format

Each themed quiz contains **60 questions in three rounds**:

1. Easy — 20 questions
2. Medium — 20 questions
3. Hard — 20 questions

Planned quiz themes:

- The Ultimate Harry Potter Quiz
- Marvel: Heroes, Villains & Infinity
- Bonjour, France!
- Passport Please! (world travel and geography)
- Screen Time (film and television)
- The 90s & 2000s Time Machine

The existing generic **Brains & Embarrassment** quiz remains available.

## Quest production rules

- Build five curated groups with 25 quest placements per group. A strong quest
  may appear in more than one group; repeated placements reuse the same game and
  cover rather than creating duplicates.
- Use photo, video, and text submissions. Most videos should be 10–20 seconds,
  with 15–30 seconds acceptable and 60 seconds the absolute maximum.
- Binary challenges use fixed points: approve awards all points; reject awards
  zero. Subjective creativity/performance challenges use a judged range,
  normally 50–250 points.
- Most fixed challenges award 50–100 points. Higher scores are reserved for
  harder, longer, cross-team, or especially creative tasks.
- Every unique game gets a cover image in its group's colour language. Labels
  describe suitability such as `indoor`, `outdoor`, `summer`, `winter`,
  `office`, `physical`, `collaborative`, `creative`, `photo`, `video`, and
  `text`.

## Cover-image direction

- Covers should be energetic editorial illustrations rather than generic stock
  photos, with large readable titles and no small instructional text.
- Keep a consistent RallyHub family while giving each group its own palette.
- Quiz covers should visually communicate the theme without copying protected
  logos, film stills, actors' likenesses, or franchise artwork. Use original,
  theme-adjacent visual language.
- Generate covers only when the content itself is approved next week.
