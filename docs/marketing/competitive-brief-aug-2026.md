# Competitive brief: who we are up against

Stonewire Technologies · RallyHub · August 2026

Researched 9 August 2026 from the competitors' live sites, pricing pages, product
applications, company registries and third-party review platforms. All quotes are verbatim.
Anything I could not confirm is marked **Unverified**.

---

## 1. Executive summary

Six competitors matter in the European team-event software market. They fall into three
groups, and only one group is genuinely playing our game.

**No competitor combines all four of these in one product:** mixed-format events,
per-client-per-event branding, a live facilitator control room, and an in-event physical
store. Taken individually, every one of those is contested.

**Our only uncontested advantage is true white-label multi-tenancy** — an agency running
events on their own subdomain, under their own brand, with our name removed. Nobody else
offers it, and our closest feature competitor structurally cannot.

**Our three real exposures** are no offline play, no third-party reviews at all, and the
slowest buyer entry in the category.

### Threat ranking

| Rank | Competitor | Country | Why they matter | Threat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **espoto** | Germany | The only one building the same product. Already sells the live control room at double the base price, so the market has proved it pays for our core bet. | High |
| 2 | **Loquiz** | Estonia | Owns the discovery journey. 101 reviews at 4.7, a blog twice a week, an instant free trial. Buyers decide before we exist to them. | High |
| 3 | **mooveTEAM** | Spain | Chases exactly our buyer, the reselling agency, and removes all first-event risk. Already localised into Bulgarian and Polish. | High |

## 2. The landscape: three kinds of competitor

**Event-software platforms** — espoto, mooveTEAM. They sell the software; agencies run the
events. This is our category and our buyer.

**Agencies with a platform** — TeamingLabs. Runs events for a living and licenses its
software to other agencies on the side. Closest feature match, no commercial front door.

**DIY game builders** — Loquiz, Actionbound, Cluetivity. Strong distribution and self-serve
entry, weak or entirely absent live-event layer. They win the search, not the room.

## 3. espoto — Germany

> "Software for digital scavenger hunts, city rallies, escape & serious games."
> "With our Serious Games software, you can create a digital scavenger hunt, an interactive
> city rally, an exciting escape game, a quiz, and many other indoor and outdoor adventures
> in just a few minutes—without any programming."

**CTA:** "Start now for free »"

### Who they sell to and how
German company (espoto GmbH), German servers, GDPR-forward. Sells to team-building
organisers, tourism operators, education and corporates. Fully self-serve on a free trial
with published pricing and no sales gate. They also run paid operator training, which
implies a partner ecosystem without a formal franchise.

### Pricing — per player, no subscription
- **Basic event: €2.50 per player** (reduced from €3.50 as a 2026 offer). Includes event
  branding, dynamic layout, custom pages, all task types, custom maps and labels.
- **Professional: €5.00 per player** (reduced from €7.00). Adds operator calls, chats,
  gadgets, dynamic review, team overview.
- Tourism non-commercial €29.99/month; tourism commercial 20% of ticket sales.
- Education flat rate €49.99/month, or €1.50 per player.
- No setup fees, no fixed monthly cost for team events.

### Capabilities
Mixed formats in a single event, explicitly marketed. Per-event client branding included in
the cheapest paid tier. **A live operator layer sold as the paid upgrade**: their copy says
an operator "can interact directly with participants, give them tips or adjust the
difficulty level spontaneously". Roles are tiered as supervisor, event manager, operator and
player. Browser web app with no install, plus native apps. Live rankings.

**They do not have:** a physical store (their "gadgets" are virtual items used between
teams), white-label, or an own-domain option. A dedicated big-screen audience display is
**unverified**.

### Recent activity — the best cadence in the field
- 9 Jul 2026: "Heart of the North Sea" location-based game for East Frisia
- 29 Jun 2026: "Tesouro" family treasure hunt, Portugal
- 3 Mar 2026: PIN login, hideable spots, automatic high scores
- 14 Jan 2026: video task recordings extended to 180 seconds
- Dec 2025: online editor overhaul. Oct 2025: hint system redesign

### Marketing
The strongest technical SEO structure of the field: separate ranking pages per app, per
sector, a support knowledge base with glossary pages, a training catalogue and a dated news
archive. **Real product screenshots on site.** No Capterra, G2 or GetApp listing found.

### Strength and weakness
**Strongest:** they have productised the facilitator control room and proved buyers pay
double for it. That validates our core bet and removes any claim of exclusivity on it.

**Weakest:** pure per-player pricing means no recurring revenue and no reason to be in an
agency's toolchain between events. An agency running one 40-person event pays €200 and owes
nothing further. Our subscription model is stickier, if we can justify the floor.

## 4. Loquiz — Estonia

> "The best tool to gamify your events"
> "Transform your idea into real-world games for events, tourism or education."

**CTA:** "Start for free"

### Who they sell to and how
Fully self-serve and product-led. Event professionals, HR and leadership teams, tourism
operators, museums, education. No sales gate anywhere. Claims 45+ countries, 20,000+ games
developed, 400+ free-to-use tasks and templates.

### Pricing — per device, subscription
- **Free trial:** 14 days, 10 devices, 1 simultaneous game, 10 tasks per game
- **Monthly:** €9/month base **plus €9 per device per month**, up to 200 devices
- **Yearly:** €600/year base **plus €5 per device per year**
- **Custom:** unlimited devices, results API, custom support
- Their definition, verbatim: "One Player is one activated device."

### Capabilities
Tours and city guides, event games, HR and team-building games, educational games, museum
guides, audio tours, remote and hybrid events. Block-based drag-and-drop creator, 400+ ready
tasks. Custom branding on paid tiers. Ticketing and payments API, results API, a public
template marketplace, native iOS and Android apps.

**They do not have:** a live facilitator control panel (**not mentioned anywhere on the
features page**; their API and analytics suggest post-hoc reporting), an audience display,
or a physical store.

### Recent activity
- **11 Mar 2026: a new Creator interface became the default**; the old platform is now
  "Loquiz Legacy". Mobile-friendly editing, redesigned flow, tutorial series.
- 10 Jul 2026: "10 underrated features of the new Creator"
- 4 Aug 2026: new competitive "Match" template with scoring and rankings
- Ongoing: QR code integration, AI-assisted game creation

### Marketing — the strongest content operation in the field
Blog roughly twice a week (posts dated 4 Aug, 30 Jul, 24 Jul, 23 Jul, 16 Jul, 10 Jul 2026),
a Loquiz Academy with tutorial videos, a template library, and monetisation-angle posts
aimed at operators. They also chase AI-adjacent search terms.

**Review presence, the best of the field:** **Capterra 4.7 from 101 reviews** (96% positive,
0% negative). **G2 4.8 from 2 reviews.** Capterra positions them against Kahoot, Goosechase
and Zelos.

### Strength and weakness
**Strongest:** distribution. Reviews, content cadence and a frictionless trial mean a buyer
has found, tried and formed an opinion before we exist to them.

**Weakest:** **no live facilitator layer at all.** It is a game-building tool, not an
event-running tool. Once the game starts, the organiser is a spectator. That is precisely
the gap we fill.

**Watch:** their €5 per device per year makes our €10 per extra device per event look
expensive on a comparison page, whatever the real difference in value.

## 5. mooveTEAM — Spain

> "Level up your business with highly engaging and profitable mobile gamified experiences"
> "mooveTEAM is an event gamification platform that allows event planners to create
> customizable experiences for corporate events, including GPS-based treasure hunts, escape
> games, indoor quizzes, and more."

**CTA:** "Request a Demo Now" (contact form only, no calendar)

### Who they sell to and how
Squarely B2B2B, aimed at event agencies and planners. Positioning is about the partner's
margin, not the participant's fun. Three commercial routes: **Creator Pro** (build your own),
**Event Pro** (reseller, sell pre-built games), and a done-for-you game design service.
Partner portal, "Become a Partner" application, **17+ named partners** across Spain, Italy,
Poland, France, Greece, Slovenia, UK, Netherlands, USA and Mexico. Offices in Spain, France,
Poland, UK/Ireland, Mexico and USA.

### Pricing — credits, not subscription
- **Free to build.** No cost to create and edit, unlimited demo plays. **You pay only when
  you sell to a client.**
- Charging unit: **1 credit = 1 device.** Credits never expire.
- Standard team building (shared iPad, 1 team = 1 credit): **€55** single, €50 at 25+,
  €45 at 50+, €35 at 100+, €30 at 500+
- Remote/BYOD (1 player = 1 device = 1 credit): **€10** single, €5 at 200+, €4 at 1000+
- Education: €15 single, €10 at 100+, €8 at 200+
- Premium customisation (logo, map customisation) requires a partnership upgrade.
  **The cost of that upgrade is not published.**

### Capabilities
GPS treasure hunts, escape games, indoor quizzes, iPad rallies, fitness and step-count
challenges, murder mysteries, virtual activities, minigames (puzzles, guessing, hangman,
matching), photo and video challenges, plus an AR app (mooveXR).

Multiple game types in one event is implied by their case studies but not sold as a headline
capability. Per-event branding exists but is **gated behind the paid partnership upgrade**.
Real-time team tracking is referenced but there is no marketed control-room product
(**unverified**). No audience display found. No physical store.

### Recent activity — the weakest cadence in the field
Most recent dated product release is **11 May 2025** (bulk-editing view, plus an AR app
hotfix). Recent non-product news includes an Italian representative and a partner case
study. **No dated 2026 product release found.**

### Marketing
Sector landing pages, a public game library, a blog, FAQs. Partner-led content is the
dominant format. Site in **English, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish and Bulgarian** — the
only competitor localised into two of our target markets. No review-site listings found.

### Strength and weakness
**Strongest:** zero-risk economics for an agency. Build and demo unlimited games for free,
pay only when a client has already paid you. That is the single sharpest answer to the
objection our €20 to €200 monthly floor creates for an agency running two events a year.

**Weakest:** credits scale badly exactly where agencies care. A 100-person BYOD event costs
€500 to €1,000 in credits with no subscription cap. Their public product evidence is
fifteen months stale, which makes the platform look under-maintained next to espoto and
Loquiz shipping monthly.

## 6. TeamingLabs — Spain

> "Teaming App es una solución integral diseñada para otras empresas de team building que
> buscan ampliar y optimizar sus servicios con tecnología avanzada."
> "Transformamos grupos de personas en equipos" / "We transform groups of people into teams"

### What they are
A Spanish team-building **agency** (TEAMING LABS SL, Barcelona, founded 2019, incorporated
2021) that also licenses its platform to other agencies under a programme called **Teaming
Partners**. Eight named staff, **one developer**. No outside funding found, 2019 to 2026.
The founders also run a physical arcade venue opened in late 2023.

### The platform is real
Their app is a live multi-tenant platform with organisation switching and credit metering.
Reading its routes and interface strings, it covers:

- **Live gameplay:** games, musical bingo, player view, team pick, event lobby
- **Facilitator control:** dedicated facilitator URL, lock and unlock all challenges,
  pause and resume, manual scoring with categories, facilitator-participant chat
- **Audience display:** shareable ranking link, configurable public-screen title, show or
  hide rules, reveal final ranking, per-challenge and cooperative rankings
- **Branding:** client logo, event logo, primary, secondary and timer colours, plus separate
  colours for generated PDFs
- **Agency back office:** clients, bookings, quotes, budgets, calendar, invoices, expense
  invoices, staff assignment, facilitator accept and reject, monthly facilitator payroll
  summaries
- **Offline:** maps and media cached to the device

Interface ships in Spanish, English and Catalan.

### The store — deeper than ours
This is the finding that most affects our positioning. Their in-event store models stock
levels, per-team purchase limits, physical collection **and return of materials**, and
purchases that **unlock the next challenge** rather than simply spending points. Verbatim
strings include "Item purchased, you can now go pick it up at the store", "Only one purchase
per team", "Return the material to the store to select the next challenge", "Stock Shop".

**RallyHub's store is not a unique differentiator.** Any marketing claiming otherwise is
false and has been corrected on our site.

### Activity catalogue
30+ activities. App-based ones with direct RallyHub overlap: **Music Bingo Revolution**
(digital card, live host, plus photo tasks, video-clip quizzes and lyric completion),
**Sentido Común** (TV-style quiz with a roulette draw), **Clash of Teams** (tablet quizzes
earn coins, teams buy physical challenges), **iPad Hunt / Geoquest** (geolocated route with
photo and video). Formats with no RallyHub equivalent: **Cluedo AI** (interrogate
AI-generated suspects), **SYNERGYK** (proprietary hardware, generates a team competency
report), **Collabo** (collective 3D city rendered live on a big screen).

Challenge types in their engine: quiz, multiple choice, multi-answer, photo, video,
free-text with fuzzy matching, staff-password gates, card ordering, timeline, guess-the-price
slider, construction, bingo. No dedicated puzzle type.

### Pricing
**Nothing published for the platform.** No price, plan, per-device fee or partner rate card
anywhere. The only visible monetisation is an internal credits system.

**Known privately: €30 per team.** A ten-team event costs €300, against €169 for a
comparable RallyHub Pro event. We are the cheaper platform at every size checked.

For events they do publish figures: activity from **€25 to €80 per person**, venue €10 to
€30, catering from €30, full event **€65 to €140 per person**.

**FUNDAE is their hardest-to-match weapon.** They are an accredited Spanish training entity:
"our clients can subsidise up to **70% of the cost**", and they file all the paperwork. No
software vendor can offer this.

### Marketing
183 indexed Spanish pages including 40+ city pages and city-by-category crosses, 50+ Academy
articles targeting commercial intent. English is 28 pages of activity descriptions with no
blog, no case studies, and **the Teaming App page is excluded from their own English
sitemap**.

**They are ahead of nearly everyone on AI search.** Their robots.txt points to a 12KB
`llms.txt` and a 28KB `llms-full.txt`: a briefing document for AI assistants with recommended
brand descriptions, comparison tables, per-city recommendations and a section forbidding
models from fabricating prices.

**Proof:** 15 structured case studies. Named clients include **ONCE** (200 people, Music
Bingo adapted for visually impaired participants), **TD Synnex**, and **Centre Nacional
d'Anàlisi Genòmica** (repeat client). Social is thin: LinkedIn 340 followers, Instagram only.

**How they win deals:** experiential selling. **Teaming Day**, an annual showcase where
agencies and DMCs play the games as participants, second edition held 2026 in Barcelona.
Plus a 24-hour quote promise.

### Strength and weakness
**Strongest:** they own Spain. Deep Spanish-language search, agency and DMC relationships
built through a live showcase, named enterprise clients, FUNDAE, and their first activity
already licensed into a 90-country agency network.

**Weakest:** nobody can buy the software. No price, no signup, no trial, no partner enquiry
form, and **zero third-party mentions of "Teaming App" anywhere online**. One developer.
And **no white-label**: no custom domains, no tenant subdomains, no de-branding, so an agency
adopting it visibly runs a competitor's product.

### The structural flaw we should attack
They ask Spanish agencies to license software from the agency bidding against them for the
same accounts. That caps their partner business permanently and it is the one thing they
cannot fix without abandoning their main revenue line.

## 7. Actionbound — Germany

> "Create mobile adventures and interactive guides for smartphones and tablets"

Their H1 is a rotating keyword carousel rather than a proposition.

### Who they sell to and how
Berlin-based, education-first with a corporate side business. Three segments: Professional
(team building, trainings, company tours), Education (campus tours, school trips, museums,
libraries), Personal (free, non-commercial). Fully self-serve with online checkout.

### Pricing — prepaid player blocks
- **Personal: free**, non-commercial
- PRO, by number of Bound players: 50 for **€350**, 100 for €600, 200 for €900, 500 for
  €1,500, and "Enterprise Flat" up to 2,500 for **€2,250**
- The Enterprise Flat "can only be used at one location" and unused players do not transfer
- **Premium add-ons, annual:** Multi-language €350, **Bound Styler (branding) €550**
- Effective cost falls from €7 per player at the smallest block to €0.90 at the largest
- EDU pricing is discounted but **not published** (**unverified**)

### Capabilities
Digital scavenger hunts, GPS treasure hunts, tablet tours, escape games, guided walks.
Elements: quiz, missions, tournaments, QR codes, GPS, maps, compass, rewards, points,
countdown, surveys, feedback. Offline play is a genuine strength for their education market.

**They do not have:** any live layer. Bounds are self-guided and asynchronous, results
arrive on a page after play. Branding requires the €550/year add-on. A global ranking shows
when a Bound finishes, but nothing live.

### Scale and recent activity
Their 2025 review reports **1.3 million players in 2025 across 200+ countries and 90,500 new
Bounds created**. Team restructure with a new customer success function. **A full app and
website redesign is announced for 2026.** Recent updates: in-app language switching, global
ranking on finish, in-house map server.

### Marketing
Broad but education-weighted: blog, Academy, courses, consulting, a done-for-you production
service, help, FAQ, manual, forum and a changelog. Very heavy German-language footprint.
**Real product screenshots on site.** No Capterra, G2 or GetApp listing found; app-store
reviews are the main public signal and are mixed, with recurring complaints about GPS
accuracy and stability (low-confidence aggregation).

### Strength and weakness
**Strongest:** volume and trust. 1.3 million players, a free tier that seeds the funnel, and
self-serve checkout with no sales friction. They own the education entry point, and education
buyers become corporate buyers.

**Weakest:** no live layer at all. For an organiser running a room full of teams, that is a
structural gap, not a missing feature. Branding paywalled at €550/year is also awkward for
agencies rebranding per client.

## 8. Cluetivity — Germany

> "Digital Escape Games" · "Expand Your Business with Outdoor, Indoor and Mobile Escape Games."
> "Get a Cluetivity License and Become Market Leader in Your Area"

### What they are
Not SaaS. A **territorial licence business**, Berlin-based, founded 2010, claiming "more
than 250 partners in over 40 countries". They sell to escape rooms, paintball and laser tag
venues, tour operators, camping sites and education. The pitch is "become market leader in
your area", not "run a better event".

### Pricing — per city licence
- Small city (0 to 50,000 inhabitants): **€199/month or €1,990/year**
- Medium city (50,000 to 300,000): **€299/month or €2,990/year**
- Large city (300,000+): **€399/month or €3,990/year**
- All tiers include 5 ready-to-play games, unlimited events, **territorial exclusivity**,
  training, marketing material and support (their Capterra listing says 6 games; the pricing
  page says 5 — **discrepancy**)
- Custom game development from **€4,990**; EDU licence from €990/year
- 12-month term. "Only 1 license partner per city."
- **Renewal pricing "adjusts based on estimated revenue (up to 15% of earnings)."** The
  advertised flat rate is not flat after year one, and all headline figures are floors.

### Capabilities
Six named titles: Treasure Hunters, Operation Mindfall, Magic Portal, Blackout, Christmas
Adventures, Bachelor Royale. Mechanics include text, audio and video riddles, multiple
choice, 2D minigames, photo and video challenges, and AR puzzles with virtual avatars.

**A real-time operator dashboard** with team location and progress tracking — one of the
stronger live layers in the field. White-label exists at partner level, not per client per
event. **iPads are required**, a real cost the licensee carries. No physical store. No
mixed-format event programmes.

### Recent activity
**Nothing dated found in the last twelve months.** The six-game catalogue appears stable.

### Marketing
Game-title and industry pages are the SEO backbone, weighted towards partner recruitment
rather than end-buyer content. A revenue calculator is used as a lead-gen device. Site in
English, German, Spanish, French and Italian; games support 16+ languages.

**Capterra 4.8 from 5 reviews** (starting price listed as €1,990/year), plus GetApp and
SoftwareAdvice listings. Low volume, so the rating carries little weight.

### Strength and weakness
**Strongest:** territorial exclusivity converts a software purchase into a local monopoly,
which is an easier sell to an agency owner than a per-event fee, and it locks competitors
out of that city for twelve months. We cannot match it without changing our business model.

**Weakest:** the catalogue is fixed and the format is one-note. Five or six pre-built escape
games, no mixed programmes, no client-specific event branding, mandatory iPad hardware, and
a renewal clause taking up to 15% of the partner's earnings — a reasonable wedge for us to
attack in sales conversations.

## 11. Where we stand

### Real advantages
- **True white-label multi-tenancy.** Their domain, their brand, our name gone. No competitor
  offers it, and the closest one structurally cannot.
- **The combination.** Mixed-format events, per-client branding, a live control room and a
  physical store in one product. Each is contested; together, unmatched.
- **Browser-first, nothing to install.** Against Cluetivity's mandatory iPads and mooveTEAM's
  per-device credits.
- **Shipping speed.** Against one developer at TeamingLabs and a fifteen-month release gap at
  mooveTEAM.
- **Uncontested markets.** Poland, the Baltics and Malta have no platform incumbent, though
  mooveTEAM has begun localising into Polish and Bulgarian.
- **Price at scale.** Cheaper than TeamingLabs at every size checked (€169 vs €300 for a
  ten-team event).

### Where we are exposed
- **No offline play.** A realtime browser app fails at a rural offsite. Five competitors ship it.
- **No social proof.** No reviews, no client logos, no case studies. Loquiz has 101 reviews
  and adds more weekly.
- **The slowest start in the category.** espoto, Loquiz and Actionbound all let a buyer be
  playing within a minute.
- **espoto is building our roadmap** and already charges a premium for the live layer.
- **Spain is effectively closed** without a white-label-led pitch, between TeamingLabs'
  search dominance and FUNDAE.
- **Germany is espoto and Actionbound's home turf.**

## 12. Recommended actions

### This month
- **Reposition on white-label.** "The platform, not the agency. We never show up at your
  client's event." No competitor can answer it.
- **Get listed on Capterra, G2 and GetApp** and seed reviews from pilot clients. espoto,
  mooveTEAM and Actionbound are all absent, so the shelf is half empty.
- **Publish an llms.txt.** TeamingLabs is quietly winning AI-mediated search. Roughly a day
  of work.
- **Add a client-branding link** so the end client sets their own logo and colours. A day of
  work, and it wins demos.

### This quarter
- **Decide on offline.** Either build a cached mode or explicitly sell indoor and connected
  venues. Doing neither loses deals quietly.
- **Add a free build-and-preview tier.** mooveTEAM proves it removes the risk from an
  agency's first event, and it is their sharpest weapon against us.
- **Go past TeamingLabs on the store:** stock control, per-team limits, and purchases that
  unlock stages.
- **Choose the beachhead deliberately.** Poland, the Baltics and Malta are open. Spain needs
  the white-label pitch or nothing.

### Tripwire
If espoto adds a physical-store mechanic or true white-label, our combination advantage
collapses to a single feature. They ship monthly, so re-check quarterly.
